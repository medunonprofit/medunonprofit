Why it matters

Your time here becomes someone else's confidence in a crisis

Emergencies can happen anytime, anywhere. Before professional help arrives, the knowledge in the room is what makes the difference. By teaching those skills from a young age, we're building communities that know how to respond instead of freezing up, and a generation of leaders who educate, support, and protect the people around them.
