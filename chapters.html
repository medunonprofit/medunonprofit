Starting a chapter

Three steps to bring MEDU to your school

STEP 01

Gather interest

Find a few classmates who care about healthcare access or emergency readiness. Most chapters start with three to five people.

STEP 02

Apply

Fill out the chapter application so we can connect you with the founding board and existing chapter leaders for support.

STEP 03

Launch

Pick your first project from the four pillars, run it, and start logging volunteer hours for your members.
