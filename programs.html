How we action this

Four pillars, one direction.

Every chapter runs on the same four-part model. It starts with being ready to respond, moves through creating and discovering, and closes by uniting members across the world so no chapter works alone.

01 · READY

Ready

We train students in basic first aid and emergency bystander response, from hands-only CPR basics to recognizing when a situation needs 911. The goal isn't to replace EMS, it's to close the gap before EMS arrives, so help never has to wait for someone else to show up.

02 · CREATE

Create

We craft anatomical therapy flowers out of pipe cleaners and design visual health guides for local shelters and clinic waiting rooms, work that's hands-on, low-cost, and genuinely useful to the people who receive it.

03 · DISCOVER

Discover

We map out medical career pathways for members who are curious but don't know where to start, and raise scholarship funds so cost is never the reason a student can't chase a healthcare career.

04 · UNITE

Unite

We pair local members with international buddies so life-saving skills and chapter ideas travel across borders and time zones instead of staying stuck in one school.
